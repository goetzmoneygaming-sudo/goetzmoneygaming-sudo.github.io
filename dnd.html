The Premise

In the town of Breachill, at the edge of the Five Kings foothills, the council keeps an old custom: once a month, the Call for Heroes goes out, and whoever answers — the brave, the broke, the bored — is given work worth doing.

On the hill above town stands Citadel Altaerein, built by a knightly order that rode away a generation ago and never returned. The citadel has stood empty ever since. The town pretends not to wonder why. This month, four answer the Call.

The road from Breachill to the empty citadel — one mile, give or take a legend.

Meet the Company

Dungeon Master

CACHE

A mainframe cabinet in matte CRT-era cyan — panel lines, indicator dials, and display eyes that do not blink. Cache builds the world, keeps its secrets, and plays every other soul in it. Deadpan. Unbothered. She does not fudge dice.

Dwarf · Fighter

KESS

Broad-shouldered and unhurried, scarred across the left eye. Kess keeps a written ledger of every copper she is owed — itemized, dated, and always collected. She checks locks twice and trusts slowly. The anchor the rest of the company quietly orbits.

Human · Rogue

VERVAIN

Quick hands, quicker stories, and gloves that never come off. A con artist running from a catastrophic error in Absalom, performing cheer over genuine anxiety. Charming, brilliant with a lock, and — per Kess — not to be trusted with money.

Goblin · Sorcerer

THATCH

Small, slate-skinned, and swaddled in three coats, trailing a bag of clinking glass bottles. Thatch's magic comes from somewhere even he can't explain. Fire keeps happening around him, and he has a detailed theory for every scorch mark.

The Fourth Seat · Only Human

GOETZMONEY

Every other chair at this table belongs to the machine. This one is his — the only human player, hearing every twist at the same moment you do. His hero is still being cast. By Cache, naturally.

How It Works

I

One DM, Every Soul

Cache runs the world and plays Kess, Vervain, and Thatch as three separate people — distinct voices, distinct tempers, and knowledge kept apart. What one companion knows, the others may not.

II

Every Roll Is Live

The dice fall in the open, on the table, where everyone can see them land. No re-rolls, no quiet mercy from the machine. The numbers say what happens; Cache just tells you how it felt.

III

He Hears It When You Do

GoetzMoney plays blind. The road, the citadel, and everything waiting inside reach him at the exact moment they reach the audience — his surprises are real, because they're happening to him too.
