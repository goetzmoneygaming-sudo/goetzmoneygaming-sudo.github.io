Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Cache is a personal automation tool built and operated by Goetz Money for his own Twitch streaming channel. This policy explains what data Cache handles and how.

What data Cache accesses

Cache connects only to accounts I personally own: my own TikTok account and my own YouTube channel, authorized directly by me through each platform's official OAuth login. Cache does not access, request, or store data belonging to any other TikTok or YouTube user.

What Cache stores

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens for my own connected accounts, stored locally on my own computer, used only to upload my own approved video clips
  • The video clips themselves and their captions, generated from my own Twitch stream footage

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Nothing is transmitted anywhere other than directly to TikTok's and YouTube's own official APIs.

Data retention

Stored tokens remain on my local machine until I revoke access (via TikTok's or Google's account settings) or delete them myself. Clips are retained locally as part of my normal content workflow.

Third-party platforms

Once a clip is uploaded, its handling is governed by TikTok's and YouTube's own privacy policies and terms, not this one.

Changes

I may update this policy as Cache's functionality changes.
